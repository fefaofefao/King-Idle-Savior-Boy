# Store listing — English (en)

**Title (max 30):** King Idle Savior Boy: RPG

**Short description (max 80):** Tap, defeat skeletons and build your guild. Your knight grows even offline!

**Full description:**

Grab your sword and save the kingdom! In **King Idle Savior Boy**, every tap makes your knight strike hordes of skeletons in a colorful 3D cartoon fantasy world.

⚔️ **TAP AND FIGHT**
Defeat enemies, collect gold and face a **boss every 10 stages**: you have 30 seconds to win!

🛡️ **BUILD YOUR GUILD**
Hire 8 heroes (Squire, Archer, Cleric, Rogue, Barbarian, Druid, Paladin and Dragoon) who fight for you all the time. Your Mage companion casts spells by your side.

✨ **POWERFUL ABILITIES**
Furious Strike, Fury and Gold Rain turn any battle around.

💎 **REBIRTH STRONGER**
Rebirth to earn Soul Crystals and unlock permanent upgrades in the Crystal Shop.

🌍 **5 ZONES**
Forest, Cave, Desert, Ice and Volcano, each with its own skeletons.

💤 **OFFLINE PROGRESS**
Your guild keeps gathering gold even when the game is closed.

📅 **SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY**
Daily rewards, 3 quests a day, about 30 achievements and the mysterious Messenger Chest.

🌐 In English, Portuguese and Spanish. Plays offline (ads need a connection).

Free to play with optional ads.
