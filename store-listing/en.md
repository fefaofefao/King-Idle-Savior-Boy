# Store listing — English (en)

**Title (max 30):** King Idle Savior Boy: RPG

**Short description (max 80):** Tap, defeat monsters and build your guild. Your hero grows even offline!

**Full description (max 4000):**

Grab your sword and save the kingdom! In **King Idle Savior Boy**, every tap makes your knight strike hordes of skeletons and creatures in a cartoon 3D fantasy world.

⚔️ **TAP, COMBO AND HIT WEAK SPOTS**
Consecutive taps build combos that pay extra gold. Watch for Weak Spots on enemies to deal huge damage. Hold your finger on the screen to attack non-stop.

👑 **EPIC BOSSES**
Face a General every 10 stages and the dreaded **Skeleton King** every 50 — he gets furious when he's losing! You have limited time to win.

🐉 **12 ENEMY TYPES**
Skeletons, Green Slime, Angry Mushroom, Shadow Bat, Stone Golem, Fire Imp, Witch and Fallen Knight, plus rare variants: **Golden** (they run away with the treasure!), **Armored** and **Giant**. Complete the Bestiary for permanent gold.

🛡️ **BUILD YOUR GUILD**
Hire 8 heroes — Squire, Archer, Cleric, Rogue, Barbarian, Druid, Paladin and Dragoon — who fight for you all the time. Your Mage companion casts spells by your side.

💎 **REBIRTH STRONGER**
Rebirth to earn Soul Crystals and permanent upgrades. The game shows how much stronger you'll get before you decide.

🏺 **RELICS AND LOOKS**
Collect 12 legendary relics dropped by bosses and level each one up to 25. Unlock 8 looks for your hero.

📜 **THE KING'S JOURNEY**
A campaign with 10 chapters and over 70 guided quests — from the Dark Forest to the Eternal Throne — followed by endless quests.

💤 **OFFLINE PROGRESS**
Your guild keeps collecting gold even when the game is closed.

📅 **SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY**
Daily rewards, 3 missions a day, dozens of achievements and the mysterious Messenger Chest.

🌐 In English, Portuguese and Spanish. Plays offline (ads need a connection).

The game is free and contains ads. Rewarded ads are always optional.
